# Mel v Russ chess

A private chess board for Mel and Russ. You can play live with camera and voice, or take turns over WhatsApp. It's hosted free on Netlify.

## Putting it online (works on a phone)

1. Open **app.netlify.com** and sign up or sign in. Use **Sign up with GitHub** so the two are linked.
2. Tap **Add new project**, then **Import an existing project**, then **GitHub**.
3. If asked, allow Netlify to see the **mel-v-russ-chess** repository.
4. Choose **mel-v-russ-chess**. Leave all the settings as they are and tap **Deploy**.
5. Wait a minute or two until it shows **Published**. Tap the site address shown at the top.
6. Optional: rename the site in **Project configuration**, then **Change project name**. For example, `mel-v-russ` gives you `mel-v-russ.netlify.app`.

## First time on the site

1. Open the site address and tap **I'm Mel**.
2. Bookmark your private link, or add it to your home screen.
3. Tap **Send Russ their link**, then hit send in WhatsApp.
4. Tap **Go to the board**, then **Start a new game**. Russ tosses the coin.

**Only open the site address yourself.** The first person to open it gets to set it up.

## Using it

Instructions are on the site's **How it works** page, at the bottom of the board.

## Cost

This runs on Netlify's free plan. Each update to the site uses 15 of the 300 free monthly credits. Playing uses almost nothing. Video goes straight between the two phones and doesn't count.
