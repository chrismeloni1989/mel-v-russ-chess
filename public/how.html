How it works

A private chess board for two. Play live when you're both on, with camera and voice if you like, or take your time and pass the turn over WhatsApp. The board always remembers where you left off.

1Your private link

Each of you has your own link. It's what tells the board whether you're Mel or Russ, so don't share yours, and only send the other player their link.

  • The first person to open the site sets it up, then sends the other person their link on WhatsApp.
  • On your phone, open your link and choose Add to Home Screen. It then works like an app.
  • Lost your link? Ask the other player to open the menu (☰) and tap Re-send their link.

2Starting a game

The very first game: whoever wants to play taps Start a new game. The other player then tosses the coin.

  • Heads: the person who started the game is White.
  • Tails: the person who tossed is White.

After that: no more coin. The starting player swaps each game, and White always moves first. Either of you can tap Start next game when a game ends.

3Live or turn by turn

There's no switch to flip. The same board does both.

Both on together

A green dot shows the other player is on the board. Moves appear on both phones within a couple of seconds. Just play.

Taking turns

Make your move, then tap the green WhatsApp button. It opens WhatsApp with "Your move" and the link ready. Just hit send.

WhatsApp can't send by itself, so you always tap send. Add the other player's number in the menu (☰) and WhatsApp opens straight to their chat.

4Moving pieces

  • Tap a piece, then tap where it should go. Dots show the allowed squares, and rings show captures.
  • Only legal moves are allowed, including castling, en passant and pawn promotion. You pick the piece when a pawn promotes.
  • The last move is highlighted so you can see what the other player did.

5Undo (5 seconds)

Tapped the wrong square? An Undo button appears for 5 seconds after your move, with a bar counting down. After that, or once the other player has replied, the move stands.

6Check, checkmate and draws

  • Check is shown automatically: the king's square glows red and the board says Check!
  • Checkmate ends the game straight away and the winner gets a point.
  • Draws are detected automatically: stalemate, not enough pieces left to mate, the same position three times, or the 50-move rule.
  • You can resign from the menu (☰). The other player gets the point.

7Captured pieces and scoreboard

The pieces each of you has taken sit next to your name. A +number shows who's ahead on material (pawn 1, knight and bishop 3, rook 5, queen 9).

The scoreboard at the top keeps the running total of wins for Mel, wins for Russ, and draws.

8Camera and voice

When you're both on the game, you can see and hear each other while you play. A small video of each of you sits next to your names, beside the board.

  1. Tap Camera & voice (under the board).
  2. The first time, your phone asks to use the camera and microphone. Tap Allow.
  3. The other player sees a green "… wants camera & voice — Join" button. When they tap it, you're connected.
  • Mic button: mute or unmute yourself.
  • Camera button: turn your camera off or on. They can still hear you.
  • Red button: end camera and voice for both of you. The game carries on.

Wi-Fi only: camera and voice need you both on Wi-Fi. On mobile data it often can't connect and says so.

Leaving the game: if you lock your phone or switch to another app, your camera stops. When you come back, tap Camera & voice again.

The video goes straight between your two phones. It isn't recorded or stored anywhere.
